# Avicenna (Ibn Sīnā)

*Era: Medieval Philosophy (c. 400 – c. 1400) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Major: Essence vs existence + Necessary Existent: for everything except God, what a thing is (essence, e.g. "horse") doesn't include that it is; existence given by cause. Chain of "possible-in-themselves" beings needs being existing by own nature — Necessary Existent (wājib al-wujūd), whose essence IS existence — God, one and simple. Aquinas' Third Way and essence/existence distinction unthinkable without this. Floating (Flying) Man: person created suddenly, floating in air, senses receiving nothing — unaware of body/world, yet would not doubt he himself exists → self-awareness primitive, soul not identical to body (compare Descartes' cogito 600 years later, with caution). Emanation + Agent Intellect: Necessary Existent eternally overflows into hierarchy of intellects/celestial spheres → world eternal for him, fought over for centuries.
- Works: Book of Healing (Kitāb al-Shifā') (c. 1020–27, vast encyclopedia logic/natural philosophy/maths/metaphysics — NOT medical book; title means soul's healing from ignorance); Book of Salvation (shorter digest, Floating Man accessible here); Canon of Medicine (c. 1025, European university textbook into 17th c.).
- Lineage: Systematised Aristotle with al-Fārābī + Neoplatonic sources; criticised by al-Ghazālī (Incoherence of the Philosophers), answered by Averroes. West: shaped Aquinas, Scotus, Latin psychology wholesale (SEP: influence on Latin psychology particularly strong).
- Why: Gave entire medieval world (Muslim, Jewish, Christian) sharpest tool (essence/existence) and one of two great proofs of God.
- Read first: SEP "Ibn Sina" metaphysics sections, then Floating Man passage + Necessary Existent argument in anthology (Adamson & Taylor; McGinnis, Classical Arabic Philosophy Anthology). Not Healing unexcerpted.
- Caution: Not "just a transmitter" — original system-builder whose theses set Latin agenda; wrote in Arabic within Islamic intellectual world.
