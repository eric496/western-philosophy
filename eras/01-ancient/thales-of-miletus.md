# Thales of Miletus

*Era: Ancient Philosophy (c. 600 BCE – c. 270 CE) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Major: Everything's archē is water — one underlying natural principle explains the variety of things, by natural processes not gods. Traditions credit geometry theorems and predicting an eclipse (trad. 585 BCE) — later legend with plausible core.
- Key works: None survive; probably wrote nothing that reached us. Known via Aristotle and later reports.
- Lineage: Breaks with mythic explanation; sets up Anaximander, Anaximenes and the archē debate.
- Why it matters: Symbolic starting point of Western philosophy/science — importance is naturalism, not "water".
