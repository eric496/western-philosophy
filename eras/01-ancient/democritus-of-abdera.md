# Democritus of Abdera

*Era: Ancient Philosophy (c. 600 BCE – c. 270 CE) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Major: Atoms and the void — indivisible, solid, eternal particles differing in shape, size, arrangement, position, moving in empty space; colour/taste/heat exist "by convention" (how atoms affect senses). Ethics of euthymia (cheerfulness/contentment) — "the Laughing Philosopher".
- Key works: Dozens of titles in antiquity, all lost; several hundred fragments survive.
- Lineage: Direct answer to Parmenides — being is many (atoms, each eternal/unchanging), "what is not" exists as void, so motion possible. Influences Epicurus; via Lucretius, early modern science.
- Why: Most striking anticipation of scientific world-picture: visible explained by invisible structures, no purpose/gods required.
- Read first: SEP/IEP Democritus + fragments; pair with Epicurus. Caution: his atoms are a philosophical hypothesis, not modern atoms.
