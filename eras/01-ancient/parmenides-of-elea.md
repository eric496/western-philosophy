# Parmenides of Elea

*Era: Ancient Philosophy (c. 600 BCE – c. 270 CE) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Major: Being is one, eternal, indivisible, changeless — change, motion, plurality impossible. You can only think/speak of what is; "what is not" is nothing, so nothing can come from it and no empty gaps can separate things. Senses deceive; only reason reaches truth. First great surviving piece of pure deductive argument.
- Key works: Poem On Nature (early–mid 5th c.); "Way of Truth" survives substantially.
- Lineage: Often read against Heraclitus; forces responses from Empedocles, Anaxagoras, atomists; Plato wrestles in Sophist and Parmenides; student Zeno's paradoxes defend him.
- Why: Father of metaphysics and logic-first philosophy.
- Read first: Guided summary (SEP/IEP) before the poem, then "Way of Truth" fragments.
