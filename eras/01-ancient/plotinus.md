# Plotinus

*Era: Ancient Philosophy (c. 600 BCE – c. 270 CE) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Major: Reality emanates (overflows like light from sun, source undiminished) from (1) The One / the Good — absolutely simple, beyond being and description; (2) Intellect (Nous) — realm of Plato's Forms, being and thinking identical; (3) Soul (Psychē) — animates/orders material world, matter last and faintest. Human souls exiled; philosophy/virtue/contemplation = return ("flight of the alone to the Alone", henōsis). Evil is privation/distance from Good, not a substance. Emanation is eternal and necessary, not creation at a moment.
- Key works: Enneads — 54 treatises (253–270 CE) arranged by student Porphyry into six sets of nine, with his Life of Plotinus as preface. Entry treatises: I.6 On Beauty, V.1 On the Three Primary Hypostases, VI.9 On the Good or the One.
- Lineage: Presents as Plato's faithful interpreter; argues with Aristotle, Stoics, Gnostics. Via Porphyry, Augustine, Pseudo-Dionysius, Arabic transmission structures medieval Christian/Islamic/Jewish metaphysics and mysticism; powers Renaissance Plato revival.
- Why: Last great system-builder of antiquity and bridge to medieval philosophy.
- Read first: Ennead I.6 On Beauty with guide/secondary first. Caution: "mystical" ≠ anti-rational — dense argumentative metaphysics.
