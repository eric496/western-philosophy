# Heraclitus of Ephesus

*Era: Ancient Philosophy (c. 600 BCE – c. 270 CE) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Major: Universal flux and unity of opposites — everything in process, opposites define/pass into each other; flux is governed by Logos, a rational order/measure most miss. Fire as emblematic basic stuff: ever-changing yet measured.
- Key works: One book, On Nature (conventional title) c. 500 BCE — survives in ~100+ aphoristic fragments.
- Lineage: Against Milesian stable substance; shapes Plato via Cratylus; Stoics adopt Logos and fire; influence on Hegel, Nietzsche.
- Why: Invents problem of identity through change (river/person stays itself while never staying same).
- Read first: The fragments with SEP/IEP entry alongside.
- Caution: Flux is measured, not chaos; "cannot step into same river twice" is later paraphrase, not verbatim quote.
