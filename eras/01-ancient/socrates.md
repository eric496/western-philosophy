# Socrates

*Era: Ancient Philosophy (c. 600 BCE – c. 270 CE) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Major: Socratic method (elenchus): "What is X?" cross-examination until definitions collapse — Socratic ignorance ("I know that I do not know"). Virtue is knowledge (no one does wrong willingly); care of the soul over wealth/reputation; "the unexamined life is not worth living".
- Key works: None — wrote nothing. Known via Plato's dialogues (early closest), Xenophon's Memorabilia, Aristophanes' The Clouds (423 BCE caricature), Aristotle. Reconstructing him is the "Socratic problem".
- Lineage: Reacts against Sophists and Athenian complacency; teacher of Plato and Antisthenes (→Cynics); trial/death = founding martyrdom.
- Why: Turns philosophy to ethics and self-examination; invents testing claims by rigorous conversation.
- Read first: Plato, Apology then Crito.
