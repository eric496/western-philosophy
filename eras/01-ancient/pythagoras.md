# Pythagoras

*Era: Ancient Philosophy (c. 600 BCE – c. 270 CE) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Major: Reality structured by number and harmony ("all is number"); soul immortal and transmigrates (metempsychosis). In his lifetime famous less as mathematician than as religious teacher on the fate of the soul, founder of a disciplined communal way of life.
- Key works: None — wrote nothing. "Pythagorean" material comes from followers (Philolaus, 5th c.) and legend; even the theorem can't be securely credited to him.
- Lineage: Drew on Milesian cosmology and Orphic ideas; hugely influenced Plato (maths, immortality of soul, cosmic harmony in Timaeus).
- Why: Fuses maths, religion, lifestyle: universe has rational numerical order (kosmos).
- Read first: SEP "Pythagoras" — separates man from myth.
