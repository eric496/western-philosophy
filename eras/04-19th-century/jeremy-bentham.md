# Jeremy Bentham

*Era: 19th-Century Philosophy (1801 – 1900) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

Founder of modern utilitarianism. Introduction to the Principles of Morals and Legislation (printed 1780, published 1789): actions/laws right insofar as they promote "greatest happiness of greatest number"; felicific calculus. Mill raised in his circle (father James Mill was close ally), inherited then humanised framework.
