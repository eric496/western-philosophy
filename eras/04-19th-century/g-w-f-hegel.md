# G.W.F. Hegel

*Era: 19th-Century Philosophy (1801 – 1900) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Major: Dialectic / Aufhebung (sublation): ideas/historical forms develop through internal contradictions; opposite generated, conflict resolved in richer position that cancels, preserves, lifts up both. Absolute Spirit (Geist): reality rational and self-developing; mind comes to know itself through nature, history, society, art, religion, philosophy ("the real is rational and the rational is real" — often misread). Lordship and Bondage ("master–slave dialectic") in Phenomenology: self-consciousness needs recognition from another; lord recognised but doesn't recognise, bondsman through fear and formative labour achieves deeper self-relation — selfhood is social. Philosophy of history: progressive realisation of freedom, "cunning of reason", embodied in ethical life (Sittlichkeit: family, civil society, state).
- Works: Phenomenology of Spirit (1807); Science of Logic (1812–16); Encyclopaedia of the Philosophical Sciences (1817, rev. 1827/1830); Elements of the Philosophy of Right (1821). Lectures on history/art/religion published posthumously from student notes.
- Lineage: Responded to Kant/Fichte/Schelling, overcoming thing-in-itself and dualisms. Influenced Young Hegelians, Feuerbach, Marx (inverted him), Kierkegaard (against), British Idealism, existentialism via Kojève, critical theory, communitarianism.
- Why: Made philosophy irretrievably historical and social.
- Read first: NOT Phenomenology cold. Secondary intro (SEP; Beiser, Pinkard), then Phenomenology Preface + Lordship and Bondage section; Philosophy of Right Preface.
- Cautions: "Thesis–antithesis–synthesis" is popularising schema (more Fichte/textbooks); Hegel's terms are abstract/negative/concrete, Aufhebung. "Real is rational" ≠ whatever exists is justified — "actuality" (Wirklichkeit) is technical subset of existence. Lordship/bondage is one passage, not whole system or literal theory of historical slavery.
