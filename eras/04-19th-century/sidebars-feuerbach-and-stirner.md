# Sidebars: Feuerbach & Stirner

*Era: 19th-Century Philosophy (1801 – 1900) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Ludwig Feuerbach (1804–1872): Essence of Christianity (1841): God = projection of human species-essence (our best collective qualities objectified, impoverishing us); theology → anthropology. Decisive for Marx (then criticised 1845 as contemplative/ahistorical) and Stirner. Read: Part I ch. 1 extracts.
- Max Stirner (1806–1856, pseud. of Johann Kaspar Schmidt): The Ego and Its Own (pub. late 1844, title page 1845): every abstraction above individual (God, Humanity, State, Morality, Feuerbach's "Man") is a "spook"; sets self-owning individual ("ownness") and voluntary "union of egoists". Marx/Engels devoted most of German Ideology to refuting him; later claimed by individualist anarchism. Read: extract + secondary.
