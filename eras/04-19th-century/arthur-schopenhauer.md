# Arthur Schopenhauer

*Era: 19th-Century Philosophy (1801 – 1900) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Major: World as Will and Representation: experienced world is representation (Vorstellung) structured by space, time, causality; inner nature/thing-in-itself is Will — blind, aimless, endless striving (not personal willpower), known most directly in body/desire. Pessimism: willing insatiable → life oscillates suffering (unfulfilled desire) and boredom (satisfied). Release via aesthetic contemplation (music highest), compassion (Mitleid) as basis of morality, and ascetic denial of the Will.
- Works: On the Fourfold Root of the Principle of Sufficient Reason (1813); The World as Will and Representation (completed 1818, published Dec 1818, title page 1819; expanded vol. 2, 1844); Parerga and Paralipomena (1851, made him famous late).
- Lineage: Built on Kant, Plato, Upanishads/Buddhism; attacked Hegel (Berlin lectures scheduled against Hegel failed). Influenced Nietzsche (early mentor, later repudiated), Wagner, Tolstoy, Freud indirectly, modern literature.
- Why: Among first major 19th-c. philosophers arguing universe at core is NOT rational — direct inversion of Hegel, same time.
- Read first: Essays/aphorisms in Parerga (Essays and Aphorisms), then WWR Book 1 and Book 4 sections.
- Cautions: Will ≠ willpower/ambition/personal god; pessimism is metaphysical argument, not gloom/advice; he argued AGAINST suicide (affirms rather than denies Will, in his terms).
