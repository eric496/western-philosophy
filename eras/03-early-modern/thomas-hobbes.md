# Thomas Hobbes

*Era: Early Modern Philosophy (c. 1600 – 1800) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Core: Everything incl. thought is matter in motion. State of nature (no common power): competitive, fearful, glory-seeking humans → war of all against all, life "solitary, poor, nasty, brutish, and short". Rational self-preservation → covenant to authorise absolute sovereign (Leviathan); contract among subjects, not with sovereign, so can't depose him. Sovereign need not be king — assembly can be Leviathan; absoluteness is point.
- Work: Leviathan (1651). Read chs. 13–17 only.
- Lineage: English Civil War context; contract tradition Locke/Rousseau invert; influenced Spinoza's politics/naturalism.
- Why: Invents modern template — derive state from individual psychology + thought experiment with no authority.
