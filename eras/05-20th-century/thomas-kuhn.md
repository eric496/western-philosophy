# Thomas Kuhn

*Era: 20th Century to Present (1900 – ) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Major: Science doesn't grow by steady accumulation. Most work is normal science (puzzle-solving inside shared paradigm: model achievement + methods, standards, worldview). Resistant puzzles = anomalies; pile up → crisis → possibly scientific revolution/paradigm shift (Copernican, Newtonian, Einsteinian). Rival paradigms incommensurable: no neutral algorithm settles choice, key terms change meaning across shift.
- Works: Copernican Revolution (1957); Structure of Scientific Revolutions (1962; 2nd ed. 1970 with clarifying Postscript — first published in Vienna Circle's own International Encyclopedia of Unified Science); among most-cited academic books ever (SEP).
- Lineage: Against positivist image; 1965 London colloquium Kuhn vs Popper (Criticism and the Growth of Knowledge, 1970). Transformed history/sociology of science; gave language "paradigm shift".
- Read first: Structure itself — primary text IS beginner text; 1970+ edition, read Postscript.
- Caution: Paradigm ≠ vibe/any big change; incommensurability ≠ scientists can't understand each other / "anything goes" (that's Feyerabend's slogan, not Kuhn's).
