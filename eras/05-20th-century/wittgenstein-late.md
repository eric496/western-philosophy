# Wittgenstein, Late

*Era: 20th Century to Present (1900 – ) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Major: Repudiated Tractatus. No single essence of language. Meaning is use (for large class of cases): words mean what they do inside language-games (ordering, joking, praying, counting) embedded in shared "form of life". Concepts like game hold by family resemblance (overlapping similarities, no one common property), not definitions. Private language argument: language referring only to private sensations impossible — no criterion for using words correctly vs merely seeming to.
- Work: Philosophical Investigations, posthumous 1953, ed./trans. Anscombe & Rhees; Part I (693 remarks) essentially ready 1946, withdrawn.
- Lineage: Above all himself; Augustine's picture of language (opening target), Frege/Russell essentialism. Influenced Austin, Ryle, Anscombe, Cavell, indirectly Kuhn.
- Why: Turned philosophy from hunting hidden logical essences to looking at how words are actually used.
- Read first: Investigations §§1–133 in short stretches; builders (§2), game (§§65–77).
- Caution: Break is real but continuity (philosophy as therapy for language confusions) also real — present as debate, treat I/II almost as two philosophers for learning.
