# Edmund Husserl

*Era: 20th Century to Present (1900 – ) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Major: Phenomenology = disciplined description of experience as lived, before theories. Intentionality: consciousness always consciousness OF something. Epoché (bracketing): suspend assumptions about whether external world exists and what science says, describe only how things appear, hunt essences. Late: Lebenswelt (life-world), pre-scientific everyday world science builds on and forgets.
- Works: Logical Investigations (1900–01, against psychologism); Ideas I (1913); Cartesian Meditations (French pub. 1931); Crisis of European Sciences (parts 1936; full posthumous 1954).
- Lineage: Intentionality from teacher Brentano; responded Descartes, Kant, naturalism (Frege's sharp review of early work part of story). Influenced Heidegger, Sartre, Beauvoir, Merleau-Ponty, Levinas, Edith Stein, later philosophy of mind/cognitive science.
- Why: Invented method ("back to the things themselves") rest of continental used or defined against.
- Read first: NOT Logical Investigations. Cartesian Meditations or Crisis selections + secondary (Zahavi, Husserl's Phenomenology). Note: born into Jewish family, faced antisemitic discrimination under Nazis; ~40,000 pages manuscripts rescued to Leuven after death — "Husserl" partly posthumous construction.
