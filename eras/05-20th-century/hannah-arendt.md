# Hannah Arendt

*Era: 20th Century to Present (1900 – ) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Major: Human Condition divides vita activa: labor (keeping bodies alive, cyclical/necessary), work (making durable world), action (speech/deeds in public among others, revealing WHO someone is). Action rests on plurality (equal enough to understand, distinct enough irreplaceable) and natality (each birth new beginning; humans can begin something new — deliberate counter to Heidegger's mortality focus). Banality of evil does NOT mean evil trivial or Eichmann innocent (she endorsed death sentence): perpetrator of enormous crimes could be thoughtless — bureaucratic joiner unable to think from another's standpoint. Origins: totalitarianism is NEW form of rule (ideology + terror making whole categories superfluous), not harsher dictatorship; "right to have rights" from Origins.
- Works: Origins of Totalitarianism (1951); Human Condition (1958); Eichmann in Jerusalem (1963, New Yorker serialisation from 1961 trial); On Revolution (1963); Life of the Mind (posthumous, unfinished).
- Lineage: Teachers Heidegger (Marburg), Husserl (semester Freiburg), doctoral supervisor Jaspers (dissertation on Augustine, 1929); Aristotle; Kant on judgement; Marx critically. Influenced democratic theory, public sphere, civil disobedience, today's refugee/statelessness debates.
- Why: Refugee from Nazism rethought evil, freedom, politics for century of totalitarianism, bureaucracy, mass displacement.
- Read first: Short essays ("What Is Freedom?" in Between Past and Future), then Human Condition Chs. I and V; Eichmann with intro/postscript context, not as slogan. Reception: Eichmann caused storm — Scholem accused lacking Ahavat Israel (love of Jewish people); harsh pages on Jewish Councils heard as blaming victims; friendships ended. Later scholarship on Eichmann's ideological antisemitism debates portrait accuracy: concept influential, not uncontested history.
