# Bertrand Russell

*Era: 20th Century to Present (1900 – ) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Major: Logicism (maths built from pure logic). Russell's Paradox (1901): set of all sets not members of themselves both is/isn't member of itself → naive set theory inconsistent; theory of types was fix. Theory of Descriptions ("On Denoting", 1905): "The present King of France is bald" analysable logically without assuming non-existent king exists. Later logical atomism: world/language analyse to simple logical atoms.
- Works: Principles of Mathematics (1903); "On Denoting" (1905); Principia Mathematica with Whitehead, 3 vols (1910, 1912, 1913); Problems of Philosophy (1912); History of Western Philosophy (1945). Nobel Literature 1950.
- Lineage: Against British Idealism (Bradley, McTaggart); built on/broke Frege; taught Wittgenstein; influenced Carnap, Vienna Circle, Ayer, whole analytic tradition.
- Why: Helped invent modern logic; showed logical analysis dissolves traditional puzzles.
- Read first: Problems of Philosophy (short, lucid, still intro textbook). NOT Principia. History is readable, opinionated — scholars dispute many verdicts; SEP for adjudication, History for pleasure/orientation, not neutral textbook.
