# Vienna Circle / Logical Positivism

*Era: 20th Century to Present (1900 – ) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

Figures: Schlick (1882–1936 founder), Carnap (1891–1970 leading theorist), Neurath, Hahn, Feigl, Waismann; Berlin: Reichenbach; British populariser Ayer.
- Core: Verification principle: statement cognitively meaningful only if true by logic/definition OR in principle verifiable by experience. Metaphysics, theology, much traditional ethics fail → not false but literally meaningless. Philosophy = logical clarification of language of science, unified science.
- Works: Carnap, Logical Structure of the World (1928); manifesto Scientific Conception of the World: The Vienna Circle (1929); Carnap, "Elimination of Metaphysics" (1932); Ayer, Language, Truth and Logic (1936).
- Lineage: Mach, Frege, Russell, Tractatus; against Kant's synthetic a priori and all metaphysics. Dispersed US/UK after 1936; Popper defined against them, Quine/Kuhn against legacy.
- Why: Even though verification principle collapsed, made logic/language/science centre of analytic philosophy.
- Read first: Ayer chs. 1, 6 as historical manifesto, not settled doctrine.
- Caution: "Principle is self-refuting, end of story" — classic objection (neither analytic nor verifiable) but positivists had replies (proposal/convention about language); report as start of argument, not knockout.
