# John Rawls

*Era: 20th Century to Present (1900 – ) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Major: Justice as fairness: choose society's rules from original position behind veil of ignorance (don't know class, race, sex, talents, religion, health). Would choose, in strict order: (1) equal basic liberties for all; (2) inequalities only if positions open to all under fair equality of opportunity AND arranged to greatest benefit of least advantaged (difference principle). Method: reflective equilibrium (adjust principles and considered judgements until cohere).
- Works: A Theory of Justice (1971; rev. 1999); Political Liberalism (1993, stability under pluralism, overlapping consensus, public reason); Law of Peoples (1999); Justice as Fairness: A Restatement (2001).
- Lineage: Against utilitarianism (maximise total welfare even at individuals' cost); revived Kant, Locke, Rousseau contract. Provoked Nozick's libertarian Anarchy, State, and Utopia (1974), communitarians (Sandel, MacIntyre), feminists (Okin), Cohen, Sen — effectively all Anglophone political philosophy since; rebirth of normative political philosophy routinely dated to this book.
- Why: Revived systematic political philosophy; liberal egalitarianism's most influential modern argument.
- Read first: NOT Theory cover-to-cover. Restatement (2001), his own shorter version; or Theory §§1–4, 11–13, 24 excerpts.
- Caution: Veil is device of representation for choosing principles (parties still use general knowledge of society), not policy of ignoring differences; difference principle permits inequalities, doesn't demand equal incomes.

Analytic thread in one line: Russell → teaches/provokes Wittgenstein → Tractatus inspires Vienna Circle → Popper then Kuhn attack positivist picture (and each other) → late Wittgenstein undermines early picture from inside → Rawls applies analytic rigour to politics.
