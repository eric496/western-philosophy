# Karl Popper

*Era: 20th Century to Present (1900 – ) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Major: Falsifiability as demarcation: theory scientific not because confirmable but because it forbids some outcome, can be falsified. No number of white swans proves all swans white; one black swan refutes. Science grows by bold conjectures and refutations, not accumulating verifications (answer to Hume on induction). Politically: fallibilism → open society (nobody knows for certain → institutions must allow criticism, removal of governments without violence). Attacked historicism (history governed by inevitable laws) in Plato, Hegel, Marx.
- Works: Logik der Forschung (1934; some listings 1935 imprint), English Logic of Scientific Discovery (1959); Open Society and Its Enemies (1945); Poverty of Historicism (articles 1944–45; book 1957); Conjectures and Refutations (1963).
- Lineage: Critical of Vienna Circle verification/meaning focus despite friendships (SEP); also Hume; Freud/Adler/Marxist history as explaining everything, risking nothing. Influenced methodology worldwide, Lakatos/Feyerabend (rebelled), liberal thought (Soros, LSE student, named Open Society Foundations after book).
- Why: Replaced "prove theory true" with "state what would show it false".
- Read first: Conjectures and Refutations ch. 1. Logic is dense; Open Society in selections.
- Caution: "One failed test and theory dies" = naive falsificationism; real tests involve auxiliary hypotheses (Duhem problem); Popper spoke of corroboration, never verification; Lakatos showed programmes rationally survive anomalies. Norm for boldness/openness, not description of lab practice.
