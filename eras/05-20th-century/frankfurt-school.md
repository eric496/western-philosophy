# Frankfurt School (Horkheimer & Adorno)

*Era: 20th Century to Present (1900 – ) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Major: Critique of instrumental reason: Enlightenment reason meant to free from fear/myth, but shrunk to calculating efficient means, treating nature/people as things to control → turns back into domination/myth ("dialectic" of Dialectic of Enlightenment). Culture industry: mass culture not made by "the masses" — film, radio, pop manufactured like commodities (standardised, profit-oriented, fake variety "pseudo-individuality"), train passivity not criticism. Critical vs traditional theory (Horkheimer 1937): theory shouldn't pretend neutrality; interdisciplinary, historically self-aware, aimed at emancipation.
- Works: Horkheimer "Traditional and Critical Theory" (1937); Horkheimer & Adorno Dialectic of Enlightenment (circulated as Philosophical Fragments 1944, published 1947, incl. "Culture Industry"); Authoritarian Personality (1950); Adorno Minima Moralia (1951); Negative Dialectics (1966).
- Lineage: Marx updated with Weber (rationalisation), Freud (submission to authority), Lukács (reification), Hegel, Kant, Nietzsche. Against positivism, fascism (Institute fled Geneva → Columbia 1934 → Los Angeles, returned Frankfurt post-1949), Stalinist dogmatism, US commercial mass culture in exile. Influenced Habermas, New Left, cultural/media studies, today's platform/attention-economy critiques.
- Why: Asked why more rational/advanced society could produce fascism, Holocaust, mind-numbing mass culture.
- Read first: "Culture Industry" chapter in excerpt + Minima Moralia dips (aphorisms). Full Dialectic needs guide.
- Caution: Culture-industry thesis is about PRODUCTION/standardisation by industry, not sneering at tastes/audiences as dupes; Adorno on jazz contested even by sympathisers.
