# Wittgenstein, Early

*Era: 20th Century to Present (1900 – ) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Major: Picture theory: meaningful proposition is picture of possible fact, shares logical form with state of affairs depicted, true if things stand as it says. Outside factual picturing (ethics, aesthetics, religion, metaphysics) cannot be said, only shown. Last line: "What we cannot speak about we must pass over in silence."
- Work: Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus — German 1921, English book 1922; only philosophy book published in lifetime; ~75 pages, 7 numbered propositions.
- Lineage: Continued/reacted against Frege/Russell (Russell's intro annoyed him). Vienna Circle read it as foundation for logical empiricism — he was not himself an empiricist.
- Why: Set analytic agenda: what can be meaningfully said, where language runs out.
- Read first: Few numbered remarks at a time WITH guide.
- Caution: Tractatus does NOT say ethics/religion worthless — almost reverse: what cannot be said was the important part (he held unwritten second part mattered most).
