# Anscombe & Foot

*Era: 20th Century to Present (1900 – ) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Anscombe: "Modern Moral Philosophy" (1958): special moral "ought"/obligation incoherent detached from divine lawgiver (like "criminal" where no criminal law); drop for thicker terms (unjust, untruthful), pause moral philosophy until action/psychology understood; coined label "consequentialism" there. Intention (1957): intentional action is one certain "Why?" applies to; same movement is different actions "under different descriptions" (pumping water / poisoning household); intention isn't inner mental extra.
- Foot: Natural goodness: judging human action defective continuous with judging living thing defective for its kind (roots not nourishing, owl can't see); virtues are what humans as life-form need to live well → moral evaluation objective/natural. Trolley problem origin: 1967 paper on abortion/double effect introduced runaway tram driver choosing kill five ahead vs steer to siding kill one, testing doing vs allowing harm; Judith Jarvis Thomson later created bystander/footbridge variants and name.
- Works: Anscombe Intention (1957, Davidson: most important treatment of action since Aristotle), "Modern Moral Philosophy" (1958). Foot "Problem of Abortion and Doctrine of Double Effect" (1967); Virtues and Vices (1978); Natural Goodness (2001).
- Lineage: Against Ayer emotivism, Hare prescriptivism, Sidgwickian utilitarianism; shaped by Aristotle, Aquinas (Anscombe), Wittgenstein (Anscombe his student, translator, literary executor). Influenced MacIntyre After Virtue (1981), Hursthouse, McDowell, action theory after Davidson, all trolley literature incl. self-driving car ethics.
- Why: Restarted serious talk of character, virtue, good human life after decades ethics shrunk to rules, consequences, expressions of attitude.
- Read first: "Modern Moral Philosophy" (~19 pages, polemical, readable) + Foot 1967 paper; then Natural Goodness opening. Intention second, with commentary.
Critical thread one line: Frankfurt asks why Enlightenment reason produced domination → Foucault power through knowledge/institutions → Derrida instability inside texts justifying them → Anscombe/Foot from INSIDE analytic rebuild ethics on character/human nature.
