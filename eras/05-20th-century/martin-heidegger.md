# Martin Heidegger

*Era: 20th Century to Present (1900 – ) · [Era overview](README.md) · [Master index](../../index.md)*

- Major: Western philosophy forgot question: what does it mean for something TO BE (Being/Sein)? Approaches via being who asks: Dasein ("being-there", human existence). Dasein not detached mind inspecting world; always already being-in-the-world — thrown into unchosen situation, caring, drifting into conformity with das Man ("the They": "one does this, one thinks that"). Owning own death (being-toward-death) breaks drift: death mine alone, always possible; facing it (authenticity) makes choices genuinely mine. Later: technology "enframes" (Question Concerning Technology, 1954) everything incl. nature as resource-on-standby.
- Works: Being and Time (1927, unfinished as published); "Letter on Humanism" (1947, "language is house of Being"); "Question Concerning Technology" (1954).
- Lineage: Husserl (teacher, Freiburg predecessor, dedicatee of Being and Time) — bracketing replaced with historically situated existence; Aristotle, Kierkegaard, Dilthey, Nietzsche. Influenced Sartre, Beauvoir, Arendt (student), Merleau-Ponty, Gadamer, Derrida, Foucault; Levinas as critic.
- Why: Re-posed question of Being through everyday existence; almost no continental after 1927 could ignore him.
- Read first: NOT Being and Time page one unaided. Basic Writings ("Letter on Humanism", "Question Concerning Technology"), then guided Being and Time Div. I (being-in-the-world), Div. II (death, temporality).
- CONTROVERSY (must not miss): Joined Nazi Party 1 May 1933, days after becoming Freiburg Rector (1933–34), remained member until 1945, never publicly apologised, banned from teaching until 1951. Black Notebooks (pub. 2014) contain antisemitic passages, renewed unresolved debate: personal/political failure separable from philosophy, or rooted in it? Neither quick answer is scholarly consensus — flag explicitly. Also authenticity ≠ "be yourself" self-help; it's owning finitude/thrown situation.
